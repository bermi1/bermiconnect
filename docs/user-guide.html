Send your first campaign in an afternoon

This guide walks a school office or a small business through Bermi Connect from sign-up to a delivery report. You don't need any technical knowledge.

Mwongozo huu unakuonyesha jinsi ya kujisajili, kuweka namba za simu, kuongeza salio na kutuma ujumbe kwa watu wengi kwa mara moja.
SchoolsFee reminders, exam results, meeting notices and emergencies to every parent.
BusinessesOffers, delivery updates, appointment reminders and payment receipts.
Churches & groupsService times, contributions and event reminders to members.

Sign up

  1. Go to app.bermiconnect.co.tz and choose Create account.
  2. Pick your account type: School, Business, Developer or Other. This only changes the examples you see.
  3. Enter your name, email, a password, your organisation's name and a phone number we can reach you on.
  4. You land on the dashboard with TZS 500 free credit, enough for 27 short messages to try things out.

You can invite colleagues later (for example the bursar and the academic master) so everyone uses the same wallet and contacts.

Jisajili kwenye app.bermiconnect.co.tz, chagua aina ya akaunti (Shule au Biashara), na utapata salio la bure la TZS 500 kuanza.

Add contacts

Open Contacts. Click Add contact to type one person in, or Import CSV to upload many at once (see below).

Type phone numbers however you normally write them: 0712 345 678, 255712345678 or +255712345678 all work, and Bermi Connect stores them in one standard form. If the same number is added twice, the existing contact is updated rather than duplicated.

Fungua "Contacts" kuweka namba moja moja au kupakia faili la CSV. Namba inaweza kuandikwa 07..., 255... au +255...; mfumo utazipanga zenyewe.

Import a CSV file

A CSV file is a simple spreadsheet. In Excel or Google Sheets, put your list in columns and choose File → Save as / Download → CSV. The first row must be the column names.

  • Any extra column (like student, class or balance) is saved too, and you can use it in messages as {{student}}.
  • Use simple column names: lowercase letters and underscores, no spaces.
  • On the import screen, choose a group to put everyone into, for example Form 4 Parents. You'll see a preview of the first rows and any problems before you confirm.
  • Rows with an invalid phone number are skipped and listed so you can fix them; the rest are imported.
Andaa faili la Excel lenye safu ya kwanza: phone, first_name, last_name (na nyingine kama student, class). Hifadhi kama CSV kisha pakia. Safu za ziada zinaweza kutumika ndani ya ujumbe.

Groups

A group is a named list you send to, such as Form 4 Parents, Teaching Staff or VIP Customers. One person can be in several groups; if you send to two groups at once, someone in both still gets only one message.

Create groups under Contacts → Groups → New group. Add people by importing a CSV into the group, or by ticking contacts in the table and choosing Add to group. Deleting a group never deletes the contacts in it.

A tip for schools: make one group per class and one per form (for example Form 4A Parents and Form 4 Parents), and refresh them at the start of each year.

Kikundi ni orodha yenye jina, mfano "Wazazi wa Kidato cha 4". Mtu mmoja anaweza kuwa kwenye vikundi vingi lakini atapokea ujumbe mmoja tu.

Request a sender ID

The sender ID is the name people see instead of a phone number, for example KIBASILA or DUKALAJUMA. Until yours is approved, messages go out as BERMI.

  1. Open Sender IDs → Request sender ID.
  2. Enter the name: 3 to 11 letters or numbers, no spaces or symbols. It should clearly match your organisation's name.
  3. Explain what you'll send (for example "School notices and fee reminders to parents"). Networks reject vague requests.
  4. We may ask for a letter on your letterhead or your business registration (BRELA) or school registration number.

Sender IDs are registered with each mobile network under TCRA rules, which usually takes 3 to 7 working days. Its status shows as Pending, then Approved or Rejected with a reason. Names that copy a bank, government office or another brand are not allowed.

Omba jina la mtumaji (herufi 3 hadi 11, bila nafasi) chini ya "Sender IDs". Linasajiliwa na mitandao ndani ya siku 3 hadi 7 za kazi. Kabla ya kuidhinishwa, ujumbe utatoka kama BERMI.

Top up your wallet

Bermi Connect is prepaid. Open Wallet → Top up, enter an amount in TZS and pick how to pay:

M-Pesa (Vodacom)Enter your M-Pesa number. A prompt appears on your phone; enter your PIN to confirm.
Tigo Pesa / Mixx by YasEnter your Tigo Pesa number and confirm the prompt with your PIN.
Airtel MoneyEnter your Airtel number and confirm the prompt with your PIN.
CardVisa or Mastercard through a secure payment page.

The balance updates as soon as the payment is confirmed, and every top-up and charge appears under Transactions. If money left your phone but the balance didn't change within 10 minutes, send us the transaction ID from the confirmation SMS.

Ongeza salio kupitia M-Pesa, Tigo Pesa, Airtel Money au kadi kwenye "Wallet". Utapokea ombi kwenye simu yako; weka PIN kuthibitisha.

Create a campaign

A campaign is one message sent to one or more groups. Open Campaigns → New campaign and follow the steps:

  1. Name it

    Only you see the name, for example "Term 3 fee reminder, Form 4".

  2. Choose the audience

    Tick one or more groups. You'll see how many people will receive it; anyone who has opted out is left out automatically.

  3. Write the message

    Type the message or start from a template. The counter below the box shows characters, how many SMS that is, and the total cost. Pick your sender ID.

  4. Send now or schedule

    Choose Send now, or pick a date and time (Tanzania time).

  5. Review and confirm

    Check the preview for one real contact, the number of recipients and the cost, then click Send campaign or Schedule campaign.

Kampeni: weka jina, chagua vikundi, andika ujumbe (utaona idadi ya SMS na gharama), chagua kutuma sasa au baadaye, kisha thibitisha.

Personalisation tags

Tags in double curly brackets are replaced with each person's details when the message is sent.

TagBecomes
{{first_name}}The contact's first name
{{last_name}}The contact's last name
{{student}}, {{class}}, {{balance}} …Any extra column from your CSV, using the column name

You write:

Habari {{first_name}}, ada ya {{student}} ({{class}}) iliyobaki ni TZS {{balance}}. Tafadhali lipa kabla ya 30/11. Asante.

Juma receives:

Habari Juma, ada ya Neema Juma (Form 4B) iliyobaki ni TZS 150,000. Tafadhali lipa kabla ya 30/11. Asante.

If a contact has no value for a tag, it is left blank, so check your file before sending. Long names can make one person's message longer than another's; the cost estimate already accounts for that.

Tumia {{first_name}} au jina la safu yoyote ya CSV ndani ya mabano mawili; kila mtu atapokea ujumbe wenye taarifa zake.

Schedule for later

In step 4 of a campaign, choose Schedule and pick a date and time. Times are East Africa Time. The cost is reserved from your wallet when you schedule, so make sure there's enough balance.

Until it starts, a scheduled campaign can be edited or cancelled from Campaigns. Cancelling returns the reserved amount to your wallet. Good sending hours are between 7:00 and 20:00; avoid very early or late messages to parents and customers.

Unaweza kupanga ujumbe utoke tarehe na saa fulani. Kabla haujaanza kutumwa, unaweza kuubadilisha au kuusitisha na pesa kurudi kwenye salio.

Open a campaign to watch it live. The counters show how many messages were sent, delivered and failed. The table below lists every recipient with a status:

StatusWhat it meansCharged?
QueuedWaiting to go out, or waiting for its scheduled time.Reserved
SentHanded to the mobile network; waiting for the phone to confirm.Yes
DeliveredThe phone received it.Yes
FailedThe network couldn't deliver it, usually because the phone was off for too long or the number is no longer in use.No, refunded

A phone that's switched off may show Sent for several hours, then change to Delivered when it comes back on. Click Export CSV to download the report, and use the Failed filter to find numbers to correct. Every message you've ever sent is also under Messages.

Ripoti inaonyesha nani amepokea (Delivered) na nani hajapokea (Failed). Ujumbe ulioshindwa unarudishiwa pesa. Unaweza kupakua ripoti kama CSV.

Opt-outs (STOP)

Anyone can reply STOP to stop receiving messages. Bermi Connect marks them as opted out immediately, and future campaigns skip them without charging you. Opted-out contacts show a label in Contacts.

Only switch someone back on if they ask you to. TCRA rules require that people can refuse promotional messages, so for marketing it's good practice to end with "Reply STOP to opt out". Important notices to parents of current students are fine without it.

Mtu akijibu STOP hatapokea tena ujumbe wako na hutalipishwa kwa ajili yake. Kwa matangazo ya biashara, ongeza "Jibu STOP kuacha kupokea".

What a message costs

Each SMS costs TZS 18. A normal SMS holds 160 characters. Longer messages are sent as several parts that join on the phone, and each part is charged (306 characters is 2 SMS, TZS 36).

Emoji and some special symbols (like curly quotes copied from Word) make the message hold only 70 characters per SMS. The counter turns amber when this happens; remove the symbol to bring the cost back down.

Example: a 140-character fee reminder to 400 parents is 400 SMS, which costs TZS 7,200.

SMS moja ni TZS 18 kwa herufi hadi 160. Ujumbe mrefu zaidi hulipiwa kwa kila sehemu. Emoji hupunguza herufi kuwa 70 kwa SMS moja.
